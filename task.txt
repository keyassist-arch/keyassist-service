Potential concerns worth knowing:
  - These are mid-market rates — actual rates from Myaza's quote endpoint will differ (they apply their own FX margin, which is even
  in their response: "fxMargin": "2.5"). This means the USD price stored in the DB may not exactly match what Myaza charges in
  crypto.
  - The 1-hour cache means prices can be up to an hour stale.
  - If Redis is down and the CDN fetch fails, toUsd() catches the error, logs a warning, and falls back to the raw number — so a
  ₦27,000 product would be stored as 27000.00 USD silently.

  The Myaza quote handles the FX correctly on the payment side (it takes localAmount + localCurrency and returns the real crypto
  equivalent), so the stored USD price is for display — the actual charge comes from the quote.

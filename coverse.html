    Worry-free 30 Day Returns

    We want to make sure that you’re happy with your items, so we will gladly provide an exchange, online store credit or refund your item(s) if you change your mind. 

    Due to the volume of orders during busy periods, delays of 3-5 business days upon receiving your return can be expected. Rest assured, once it has been processed, you will be notified via a confirmation email.

    Processing Returns
    1. Item(s) unworn in original packaging and in resellable condition.
    2. Item(s) unwashed/unaltered and with original tags.
    3. Lastly, we just ask that the items are returned and received within 30 days of your original order date.
    Where do I return my items?
    • Email us direct at [email protected]
    • Login to your account and click on returns under your account

    To speed up the returns process, please be sure to include your returns form.

    Please note, Online return address is not a retail location, no drop-offs please..

    I believe I've received a faulty or incorrect item

    Although we aim to provide our customers with products of the highest standard and quality, if you believe you have received a faulty/incorrect item, please contact us so we can guide you through the returns process and help resolve the problem as swiftly as possible. Please note that we’ll need a photo or two showing the fault, which will speed up the returns process for you.

    I want to exchange my items

    Log a return for an item. Once we receive the item back, we will process an exchange. Please note: This is stock dependant. If not stock is available on your exchange, a refund will be provided

    Can I receive a store credit?

    When returning your items, you can choose to receive a store credit instead of a refund. We’ll also send you an email as soon as your store credit is ready to be used. You can select to use your store credit under the payment tab in the checkout. To check your balance at any time, simply log into your Converse account and click on Store Credit. Note: Store credit is availabel to be used online only and not at any of our retail brick and morter stores.

    Step 1

    Stand up straight on a hard surface with your heel against the wall and a piece of blank paper taped to the floor, flush against the wall beneath your foot.

    Have someone mark the longest part of your foot (referred to as heel-to-toe length) on the paper with a pen or pencil, or measure yourself if necessary. Repeat with the other foot, as right and left sizes may be different.

    Step 3

    Use a ruler to measure the heel-to-toe length you marked for each foot.

    Find Your Size

    To find your clothing size, please follow the tips below:

    Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, holding the tape measure horizontally. 
    Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, holding the tape measure horizontally. 
    Hips: Place your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the tape measure horizontally.

     
    Use the chart below to determine your size, if you're in between two sizes, order the smaller size for a tighter fit or the larger size for a looser fit.
